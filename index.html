Our Story: #Tenderism

Welcome to Pastrama, a premium restaurant and barbecue destination located in the heart of Casablanca. We specialize in genuinely tender meats, signature pastrami, and mouth-watering ribs.

Our spacious dining room is designed to provide the perfect vibe, accompanied by smooth jazz music. Whether you're here for our famous Pastrama Sandwich, our slow-cooked Ribeye Barbecue, or our unique sides like Bacon Fries and Ravioli, we promise an unforgettable dining experience.
